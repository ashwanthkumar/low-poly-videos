# Baa Baa Black Sheep - Scene Descriptions

## Overview
- **Duration**: ~65 seconds
- **Characters**: Family of 5 (Father, Mother, Tommy 6yo, Lily 5yo, Baby 2yo) + Black Sheep
- **Setting**: Sunny meadow with rolling hills, a cottage in the background, wooden fence, and trees
- **Mood**: Cheerful, warm, family-friendly

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: Intro (0-3s)
Camera sweeps over a peaceful meadow. The black sheep is grazing near three wool bags. The family walks in from the left side, heading toward the sheep. The cottage and fence are visible in the background.

### Scene 2: Question (3-9s)
*"Baa, baa, black sheep, have you any wool?"*
Tommy (the son) waves at the sheep while asking the question. The sheep looks up from grazing. The camera moves closer to frame the boy and sheep together. The rest of the family watches from behind.

### Scene 3: Answer (9-15.5s)
*"Yes sir, yes sir, three bags full!"*
The sheep nods enthusiastically. Tommy and Lily clap with excitement. The camera shows the three wool bags prominently. Father nods approvingly.

### Scene 4: Master & Dame (16-23s)
*"One for the master, one for the dame"*
Father (the master) steps forward with a wave when mentioned. Then Mother (the dame) steps forward with a wave. The sheep nods at each person. Camera alternates between the parents.

### Scene 5: Little Boy (23-32s)
*"And one for the little boy who lives down the lane"*
Tommy walks closer to the sheep. Lily claps, and the baby bounces excitedly. The sheep nods at Tommy. Camera focuses on the boy approaching the sheep, then pulls back to show the whole family.

### Scene 6: Repeat (32-60s)
The song repeats with the same lyrics. The family is in a group formation. Various characters clap, wave, and nod at different times. The sheep alternates between grazing, looking up, and nodding. Camera orbits around the group with different angles.

### Scene 7: Outro (60-65s)
All family members wave goodbye to the viewer. The sheep gives final nods. Camera slowly pulls back and up to a wide shot of the meadow with the happy family.
